THE PHASE 1 PIPELINE

Built on evidence.

01 / DATA

NASA C-MAPSS

FD001 turbofan simulation data. Test engine histories stop before failure; supplied RUL labels support evaluation.

02 / FEATURES

Signals over time

Remove six constant sensors. Add rolling means and standard deviations over 5 and 10 cycles for eight sensors.

03 / MODEL

XGBoost regression

Train on RUL capped at 125 cycles. Evaluate the last observed row of each test engine and clip predictions to 0–125.
